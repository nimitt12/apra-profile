About me

Writing is a sort of magic that helps me discover new perspectives, then mould them with imagination and my pen into stories, poetries and podcasts.

I'm the Founder & CEO of Craftcart™, a handcrafted home decor, festive decor, wedding decor and gifting business, and of its handmade jewellery segment, “Aprakriti”. I laid the foundation in my late college years and have been building it since 2015.

Alongside, I've worked as a Content Marketer for brands for 9+ years. I'm indulged in several creative pursuits too: writing stories and poetries, and voicing them on my podcast, “Apravyakti”.
